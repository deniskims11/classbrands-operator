How these numbers are worked out
  • All numbers come from Triple Whale for each shop and refresh automatically about every 3 hours. "Latest day" is the last full day (US time); "today" is partial.
  • Blended spend = all ad platforms. Order revenue = gross sales − discounts + shipping + taxes (Triple Whale's Order Revenue). Blended ROAS = order revenue ÷ blended spend. MER = blended spend ÷ order revenue. NC CPA = blended spend ÷ new-customer orders.
  • Our creatives = Meta ads matching each client's tag (shown in each client's header). Meta ROAS and CPA are Meta-reported (from Triple Whale). NVP = share of the ad's visitors who were new to the site.
  • Run rate = our spend month-to-date ÷ days elapsed × days in month. Target = $150K a month per client. Projected fee = base + rate × run rate.
  • Launches count our tagged ads by their first spend day; a hit is an ad that has spent $1K+ in its life so far.
